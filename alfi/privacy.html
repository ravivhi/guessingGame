אלופי האותיות – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

אלופי האותיות ("the app") is a free Hebrew letters game for children, made by Raviv Hilleli. We do not collect personal information.

What the app does with your data

  • The app works fully offline. No data is sent from your device to us or to any third party.
  • Game progress and settings (such as score and sound on/off) are saved only in your device's local storage and can be removed by clearing the app's data.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or personal details.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of data.

Children

The app is designed for children and does not collect personal information from children or anyone else. Parents can contact us with any question.
