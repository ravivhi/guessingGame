English Quiz – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

English Quiz ("the app") is a free English vocabulary quiz made by Raviv Hilleli. The app is suitable for children and adults. We do not collect personal information.

What the app does with your data

  • Photos: Photos you choose are processed only on your device to read English words (text recognition). Photos are never uploaded or stored by us.
  • Translations: When a word has no Hebrew meaning in the photo, the English word is sent over an encrypted connection to the Google Translate web service (translate.googleapis.com) to get a Hebrew translation. If that service is unavailable, the app uses the MyMemory translation service (api.mymemory.translated.net) instead. Only the word itself is sent – no name, account, photo or device identifier. As with any internet request, the service receiving it can see the device's IP address; the privacy policies of Google and MyMemory apply to those requests.
  • Word list and translations: Your word list and the translations already looked up are saved only in your device's local storage and can be removed by clearing the app's data.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or personal details.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • No selling of data, and no sharing with third parties other than the word-translation requests described above.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else. Parents can contact us with any question.
