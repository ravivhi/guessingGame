English Quiz – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

English Quiz ("the app") is a free English vocabulary quiz made by Raviv Hilleli. The app is suitable for children and adults. We do not collect personal information.

What the app does with your data

  • Photos: Photos you choose are processed only on your device to read English words (text recognition). Photos are never uploaded or stored by us.
  • Translations: The English words found in the photo are sent over an encrypted connection to the MyMemory translation service (api.mymemory.translated.net) to get Hebrew translations. Only the words are sent – no name, account, photo or device identifier. MyMemory's own policy applies to that request.
  • Word list: Your word list is saved only in your device's local storage and can be removed by clearing the app's data.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or personal details.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else. Parents can contact us with any question.
